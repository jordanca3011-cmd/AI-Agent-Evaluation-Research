# Kingdom Tycoon - AI Agent Evaluation Case Study

## Overview

Kingdom Tycoon is a Roblox game development project used as a real-world environment for evaluating AI agent performance in creative asset workflows, world-building, visual consistency, and content organisation.

The project focuses on creating a stylised low-poly fantasy kingdom experience with multiple player-owned kingdoms, farms, buildings, resources, and modular environments.

The project evaluates whether AI agents can effectively assist with creative development tasks that require visual understanding, organisation, adaptation, and maintaining a consistent art direction across a large collection of assets.

---

## Research Purpose

This case study evaluates how effectively AI agents can assist with:

- Asset pipeline management
- Creative world-building
- Visual style consistency
- 3D asset organisation
- Blender workflows
- Large-scale content planning

---

## Development Environment

Primary tools:

- Blender
- Roblox Studio
- Python automation
- AI-assisted development workflows

---

## Project Complexity

Kingdom Tycoon involves:

- Multiple player-owned kingdoms
- Modular building systems
- Fantasy environment creation
- Low-poly asset pipelines
- Imported third-party asset collections
- Material and style unification
- Roblox-compatible asset preparation

The project requires maintaining consistency across many independent assets while ensuring they function together as a unified game world.

## AI Evaluation Areas

## 1. Creative Planning and World Building

Research question:

Can AI agents assist with designing coherent game worlds while maintaining established creative direction?

Evaluation areas:

- Environment planning
- Layout suggestions
- Gameplay space organisation
- Theme consistency
- Asset placement decisions

---

## 2. Asset Pipeline Management

Research question:

Can AI agents assist with managing large collections of 3D assets and preparing them for use in game development?

Evaluation areas:

- Asset organisation
- Naming conventions
- Collection structures
- Import workflows
- Export preparation

Measurements:

- Accuracy of organisation
- Time saved
- Errors introduced
- Required human correction

---

## 3. Visual Consistency

Research question:

Can AI agents help maintain a consistent visual style when combining assets from multiple sources?

Evaluation areas:

- Material consistency
- Colour palette decisions
- Style matching
- Adaptation of external assets

Example challenges:

- Combining different low-poly asset packs
- Standardising materials
- Maintaining a unified fantasy aesthetic

---

## 4. Human-AI Creative Collaboration

Research question:

What tasks in creative development can be effectively supported by AI while keeping human artistic direction?

Evaluation areas:

- AI suggestions versus final decisions
- Creative iteration speed
- Quality of generated solutions
- Human oversight requirements

---

## Current Challenges Observed

Areas being evaluated:

- AI understanding of visual style requirements
- Maintaining consistency across many assets
- Managing complex asset libraries

- Avoiding unsuitable asset modifications

## Research Value

Kingdom Tycoon provides a test environment for evaluating AI agents in creative software workflows.

Unlike traditional programming tasks, creative development requires understanding aesthetics, organisation, constraints, and human intent. This project helps evaluate whether AI systems can become effective collaborators in areas where success depends on both technical execution and creative judgement.
